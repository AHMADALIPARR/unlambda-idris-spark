# Commercial licensing inquiries

[![Commercial licensing](https://img.shields.io/badge/commercial-licensing_inquiries-7c3aed)](https://github.com/AHMADALIPARR)

Contact the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/AHMADALIPARR) to discuss
commercial licensing or support. An inquiry is not a license grant. Pricing,
license scope, support commitments, warranties, and permitted distribution must
be agreed in writing; no standard commercial agreement is published in v1.

Describe your intended use, deployment/distribution model, required components,
organization, and support needs. The agreement would need to identify the
covered source version, ownership/provenance, applicable third-party terms,
license duration, redistribution rights, and any support or maintenance terms.

Original project code is proprietary and source-visible, not open source. See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). Commercial arrangements can cover
only rights the licensor can grant; they do not remove third-party obligations.

v1 is a verification scaffold, with an unimplemented ATS runtime and slot-map
heap. Its commercial availability must not be described as a certified
production interpreter. The [verification report](docs/verification/ats-spark-af40c4e.md)
defines the checked component scope.
